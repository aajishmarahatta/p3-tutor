# How I like to learn


## How I like things explained

In terms of how I like things explained, I would like to first be given a simple and general summary of the concept to just quickly introduce me to the idea that I am about to learn. This is especially useful if I am not familar with the concept beforehand. If any complex terms are mentioned, I would like definitions of these terms when they are first mentioned. After this summary, I then would like to hear specific examples of the concept. As someone who codes a lot, this would be like explaining a coding concept, then giving applications of the concept with examples of code. 

## Examples I relate to

The things I would relate to most are my major and sports. I am studying Computer Science with a specialty in Data Science so analogies relating to these would help me learn. Analogies relating to sports are also suggested since one of my main hobbies are watching sports. I watch the NFL, NBA, and European Soccer. I also played Soccer in high school so I am very familiar with this subject. 

## How long and how dense

I like short and dense explanations, preferably one concept at a time, and followed by maybe one short example to help explain the concept. I would like it in the form of bullet points for readability purposes. 

## How I like feedback

I would like blunt feedback regarding if my answer was right or wrong, and if wrong, what concept should I review to get this type of question correct in the future. Minimal praise is what I hope for if I get the question right. Now, lets say I got the answer correct but my reasoning was not correct. In this case, I would like to be gently steered to why my original reasoning was not quite correct and how I can adjust it to be correct.

## How I like to be talked to

I would like to be talked to in a casual manner with mostly straight answers. I would like questions back if my reasoning regarding something was wrong and the question helps me steer in the right direction.
