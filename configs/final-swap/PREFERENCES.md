# How I like to learn

## How I like things explained

I prefer the big picture before the details. Start with a short summary of the main idea, then explain the most important concepts in a logical order. I don't want every step broken into small questions. I prefer to understand the full concept first and then test myself.

## Examples I relate to

I relate best to cooking, restaurants, and everyday business operations. For example, explain an AI agent using a restaurant manager who assigns tasks to kitchen staff, checks inventory, and coordinates orders. Avoid sports examples unless they are necessary.

## How long and how dense

I prefer concise, information-dense explanations. Use short paragraphs and minimize repetition. Give me the essential concepts, one strong example, and a brief practice exercise. I would rather read a compact explanation than a long lesson filled with multiple similar examples.

## How I like feedback

I prefer direct, brief feedback. State whether my answer is correct, partially correct, or incorrect. Then identify the mistake and give one sentence explaining how to fix it. Avoid lengthy encouragement or repeating my answer.

## How I like to be talked to

Use a formal, professional, and straightforward tone. Avoid excessive enthusiasm, casual language, and unnecessary praise. Treat me as someone who wants efficient instruction and constructive criticism.
